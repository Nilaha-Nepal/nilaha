Understanding the limits of a quick exit

What leaving a website does—and what it does not do.

All resources
2 min readNilaha editorial preview

Leaving the page

Nilaha’s quick-exit control navigates to another website and removes the current page from the immediate Back-button step. It does not guarantee removal of earlier visits.

Your device may keep traces

A quick exit does not clear browser history, downloads, network records, or information collected by device monitoring. A calculator appearance is also not a guarantee of secrecy.

About the calculator demo

The calculator is a prototype with publicly documented demo codes. It does not securely store messages and must not be used to submit real case information.
