GETTING SUPPORT

Finding support, one step at a time

A starting point for exploring your options, at your own pace.

All resources
3 min readNilaha editorial preview

You can start with a question

You do not need to have a complete account of your experience to explore the services available to you. You can begin by asking what a service offers, who it supports and how to contact it.

Find the right starting point

Use the helpline directory to find official services. Ask the provider about its scope, language options, charges and availability. Nilaha’s sample counsellor profiles are demonstrations, not bookable professionals.

Choose how to make contact

Before requesting follow-up, consider which contact method and time you are comfortable using. Calls and messages can leave traces on a shared device. You decide what to include in a request.
